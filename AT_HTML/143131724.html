Настройка селектора в APN 1404

  • В Configuration Detail выбираем подвкладку selector и в окне Export Selector заполнить данные
     

  • Ниже представлен SQL запрос для указания в Selector Options:

    SELECT 
    DISTINCT aircraft.ac_registr
FROM aircraft
LEFT JOIN ac_utilization ON ac_utilization.ac_registr = aircraft.ac_registr
    AND (sysdate-to_date('19711231','yyyymmdd')) BETWEEN (ac_utilization.sched_departure_date + ac_utilization.sched_departure_time/60/24) 
    AND (ac_utilization.sched_arrival_date + ac_utilization.sched_arrival_time/60/24)
LEFT JOIN aim_transfer_history ON aim_transfer_history.source_key = aircraft.ac_registr
    AND aim_transfer_history.aim_configno_i = 1008 
    AND aim_transfer_history.service = 'TRANSFER_AIRCRAFT' AND aim_transfer_history.address_i = 17549 
    AND sysdate-to_date('19711231','yyyymmdd') - (aim_transfer_history.trf_date + aim_transfer_history.trf_time/60/24) < 7
WHERE aircraft.status = 0
              AND ac_utilization.legno_i IS NULL
              AND aim_transfer_history.histno_i IS NULL
              AND aircraft.operator = 'AFL'
